  ↓

  └── Yes

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓
    Generate Gate Pass
       ↓
    Record Visit Date & In-Time
       ↓
    Visitor Enters
       ↓
    Update Out-Time
       ↓

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓
